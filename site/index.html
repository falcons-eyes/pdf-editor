Sign and stamp PDFs. Seal them, too.

Put your signature and stamp on a PDF you received and save it as a new file. It’s free on Mac and Windows, and the Android app is on its way. There’s no account and no internet connection, so your files never leave your device.

Three steps, and you’re done

  1. Open a PDF

    Click Open new PDF, or drop a file onto the window.

  2. Place your signature and stamp

    Drag one from the list to the right spot, then adjust its size and angle.

  3. Save a new file

    Your original stays as it is. Sealworks writes a new PDF, like Contract_signed.pdf.

Made for moments like these

  • Your landlord emailed the lease

    Sign it and send it straight back, no printer needed.

  • A new part-timer starts today

    They sign the contract on the shop tablet, and you add your stamp.

  • The quote has to go out today

    Stamp your company seal from a photo, with the white paper removed.

  • The school sent a field trip form

    Sign it neatly instead of sending a phone photo.

  • Expense forms need your approval

    Put your stamp in the same spot on every page at once.

  • You’re sending a freelance contract

    Sign and seal it, and you’ll know right away if anyone edits it later.

Seal it, and any later edit shows

Turn on Seal and save and Sealworks adds a digital signature that covers the whole finished file. If anyone changes even one character afterwards, Verify seal shows it.

A seal can’t stop anyone from editing a copy. It makes edits impossible to hide.

It isn’t a certified electronic signature under any law. Re-saving in another app breaks the seal, so keep files exactly as you received them. How to check a seal and back up your key is on the support page.
