Frequently asked questions

How do I open Sealworks the first time?

Mac App Store version (after release): it opens normally, like any app from the store.

The DMG and EXE downloads from GitHub aren't signed with paid Apple or Microsoft certificates, so macOS and Windows warn you the first time. Allow it once and it opens normally after that.

macOS (DMG)

  1. Open the DMG and drag Sealworks to the Applications folder.
  2. If the first launch says the developer can't be verified, or that macOS can't check it for malicious software, click Done (or Cancel).
  3. In Finder › Applications, right-click (Control-click) Sealworks › Open, then click Open again.
  4. If there's still no Open button, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to the message about Sealworks. (From macOS 15 Sequoia on, this is the way.)

If it says the app “is damaged and can't be opened”, that comes from the quarantine flag macOS puts on downloaded files. Paste this into Terminal, press Return, and open the app again:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Sealworks.app"

Windows

  1. Run the …-setup-x64.exe you downloaded.
  2. If a blue “Windows protected your PC” box appears, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. When setup finishes, open Sealworks from the Start menu. You can also right-click a PDF › Open with › Sealworks.

Not sure which Mac you have? Choose Apple menu › About This Mac. If it says Chip Apple M…, download the arm64 DMG. If it says Processor … Intel …, download the x64 DMG.

Where does Sealworks keep my data?

Everything stays on your computer. The Library (document copies, where you placed signatures, your signatures and stamps, settings) and your seal key are kept in this folder:

VersionFolder
Mac App Store (after release)inside the app's container, ~/Library/Containers/com.falconoon.sealworks/
macOS, downloaded from GitHub (DMG)~/Library/Application Support/PDF Editor
Windows%APPDATA%\PDF Editor

The folder keeps its old name from when the app was called PDF Editor, so updating from 1.0 keeps all your documents. Your seal key is in the seal subfolder, encrypted with a key that the macOS Keychain or Windows (DPAPI) protects.

To erase everything, quit Sealworks and delete that folder. Uninstalling the app leaves the folder in place. On macOS you can also remove the app's Keychain item in Keychain Access. Your original PDFs and the files you saved elsewhere aren't affected.

How do I back up and restore my sealer key (.p12)?

Your sealer identity (the private key and certificate used for Seal and save) lives only on your computer. If you lose or reset the computer without a backup, you'll have to make a new identity, and its fingerprint will be different.

Back up

  1. Open Sealer… and choose Back up….
  2. Choose a password (at least 6 characters) and a folder. Sealworks saves a password-protected .p12 file there.
  3. Keep the file and the password somewhere safe, for example on a USB drive or in your password manager. The password can't be recovered if you forget it.

Restore

  1. On the new computer (or in the other version of Sealworks), open Sealer… and choose Import….
  2. Pick the .p12 file and enter its password. Importing replaces the current sealer identity, so Sealworks asks you to confirm first.

After restoring your own backup, your fingerprint is the same as before. Import… also accepts a .p12 or .pfx certificate you already have.

Moving between the downloaded version and the Mac App Store version (after release)? The seal key doesn't move by itself. Back up in one and import in the other.

How do I verify a seal, and why does Acrobat say the signer isn't verified?

In Sealworks

Click Verify seal in the title bar (or on the Library screen), then drop the PDF on the dialog or click Choose file…. Checking a file never adds it to your Library. You'll see Seal intact, Modified after sealing, Seal is not valid or Not sealed, plus who sealed it, when, and the certificate fingerprint.

In Adobe Acrobat or Acrobat Reader

Open the PDF and open the Signatures panel. Acrobat shows whether the document was changed after it was signed.

It will also say the signer's identity can't be verified. That's expected. The certificate was made by Sealworks on the sender's computer, not bought from a certificate authority, so Acrobat has no outside authority to vouch for the name. The part that matters for tamper evidence is whether the document was modified.

To be sure a file came from a particular person, compare fingerprints: the sender finds theirs under Sealer… and shares it with you once, for example by phone or in person. If the fingerprint in the file matches, the file was sealed with their key.

Why did re-saving in macOS Preview break the seal?

A seal covers every byte of the file. macOS Preview rewrites the whole PDF when it saves, even when you only add a note or highlight, and it saves changes automatically. The bytes change, so Verify seal shows Modified after sealing. Other apps that re-save PDFs do the same.

Keep a sealed file exactly as you received it. If you want to annotate it, make a copy first and annotate the copy. If the seal on a file you need is broken, ask the sender for the file again.

Is a seal a certified electronic signature?

No. The seal is meant for spotting changes. It isn't a certified electronic signature under any law, and it uses a self-made certificate rather than one from a certificate authority. If a document needs a particular kind of electronic signature, check what the recipient or the law requires.

A seal also shows tampering rather than preventing it: it can't stop someone from removing the seal and sending the file on as an ordinary, unsealed PDF. If a seal matters to you, check that one is there.

How do I switch the language?

Sealworks speaks English and Korean. The first time it opens, it follows your computer's language. To switch, click English or 한국어 at the bottom right of the window (in the status bar), or use View › Language. Everything switches in place: nothing reloads, and your work stays where it is. Sealworks remembers your choice.

This website is in both languages too. Use the 한국어 link at the top of any page to switch.

How do I report a bug?

Open an issue on GitHub Issues. It helps to include:

  • Your Sealworks version (shown under About Sealworks in the status bar) and where you got it (Mac App Store, DMG or Windows).
  • Your macOS or Windows version.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps.

Issues are public, so please don't attach real documents, signatures or personal information. If the problem only shows with a private file, email support@falconoon.com instead and describe it without attaching the file.
