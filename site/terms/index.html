5. What a seal is and isn't

"Seal and save" adds a PDF digital signature (PAdES) so that any later edit to the file shows as "Modified after sealing".

  • A seal shows whether a file changed after it was sealed. It uses a certificate created on your device, not one issued by a public certificate authority. It does not prove who you are, and it is not a certified, qualified or officially recognized electronic signature.
  • Other PDF readers may show the signer as unknown or not verified. This is expected.
  • Your seal key is created and kept only on your device. We never receive it and cannot recover it. If you need it on another device, make a password-protected .p12 backup in the app and keep it safe.

6. Keeping copies

The app never changes your original PDF; it saves a new file. Your Library, signatures and seal key live only on your device, so we cannot restore them if the device is lost, reset or the app is removed. Keep copies of documents that matter.
